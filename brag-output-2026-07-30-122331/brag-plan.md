# Brag Plan: BlogForge — From Raw Material to Publishable Writing

## What is this app?

BlogForge is a local-first long-form writing workshop that develops a writer's topic, outline, notes, or sources into a coherent article, then helps shape, review, optimize, finish, and deliberately publish it in the writer's voice.

## The angle

**From something to say to something worth publishing.** Follow one article through the actual BlogForge journey. Content development and writing are the story; voice, human review, GEO, and distribution complete it.

## Hook

“Good writing rarely starts with a perfect prompt.” A sterile prompt box gives way to the real raw materials writers bring: a topic, rough outline, scattered notes, and source material.

## Key moments

- The real starting-point picker accepts a topic, outline, source material, existing draft, or blank page.
- Talk it through discovers audience, claim, tension, evidence, and conclusion before reorganizing the material into a purposeful outline.
- Compose full draft creates one coherent article rather than disconnected generated sections.
- Voice Profile and In my voice actively reshape a passage.
- Humanization Check exposes one explainable pattern, and the writer chooses whether to apply the revision.
- GEO checks answer clarity, context/entities, and retrievability alongside Proofreader, Fact-check, and Shape.
- Hero image, Atomize all, exports, and Publish to GitHub remain deliberate finishing actions.

## Outro

“From something to say, to something worth publishing—in your voice.”

## User flow worth showing

Bring topic/outline/notes/sources → develop the argument → shape a real outline → compose and revise one coherent article → review voice/humanization/editorial/GEO guidance → finish, repurpose, export, or publish.

## Tone

- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: warm editorial product film about developing thought, not generating text
- Interpretation: calm confidence, recognizable product surfaces, readable hierarchy, and precise language

## Format and Duration

- Landscape, 1920 × 1080
- Target duration: 44 seconds, synchronized to the measured narration

## Visual identity

- Canvas: `#e6efff`, `#e3f7f4`, and `#fff4e0`
- Accent: `#2f6bff` and `#16c2b3`
- Text: `#15224a`
- Editorial font: Lora
- Interface font: Inter
- Metadata font: JetBrains Mono
- Strongest recurring visual: one manuscript card carried through each stage

## Audio direction

- Narration: private on-device Kokoro voice, natural product-demo cadence
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`
- Music treatment: low bed under narration, slightly stronger at open and close, clean final fade
- SFX posture: sparse and motion matched
- Restraint rule: never compete with narration or imply autonomous publishing

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Before the prompt — 4.3s

A sterile prompt is replaced by real raw material. The hook line settles beside the input cards.

### Scene 2 — Start with what you have — 5.2s

Show all six starting modes with focus on Help me shape it, I have an outline, and I have source material.

### Scene 3 — Develop the argument — 6.3s

Talk it through questions pull out audience, claim, tension, evidence, and desired conclusion. Those answers form a new outline organized around the argument.

### Scene 4 — One coherent draft — 5.2s

Compose full draft turns the outline into one manuscript with a visible argument thread from opening through conclusion.

### Scene 5 — In the writer's voice — 5.2s

Voice Profile resolves and an inline In my voice action rewrites a selected passage.

### Scene 6 — Humanize and strengthen — 8.1s

Humanization Check opens one transparent pattern, suggested rewrite, and writer-controlled Apply action. Proofreader, Fact-check, Shape, and GEO follow, with GEO expanded into answer clarity, context/entities, and retrievability.

### Scene 7 — Finish deliberately — 5.4s

Add a hero, Atomize all, export, or initiate Publish to GitHub.

### Scene 8 — Whole-product close — 4.3s

The raw inputs, outline, article, voice, review, and finish artifacts resolve into the BlogForge lockup.

**Audio summary:** a warm narrated journey with a restrained music bed and only a few meaningful interface cues.
