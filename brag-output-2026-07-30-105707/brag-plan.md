# Brag Plan: BlogForge

## What is this app?
BlogForge is a local-first workshop for turning a writer's voice, sources, and ideas into one coherent long-form article, then refining and publishing it with the writer in control.

## The angle
**The Workshop Journey.** Contrast a generic generate button with the full craft loop BlogForge was designed to support: voice, material, structure, one coherent draft, visible editorial decisions, and intentional distribution.

## Hook (first 2–3 seconds)
“A generate button makes text. A workshop makes writing.” A generic prose block is displaced by BlogForge's working stages.

## Key moments
- A six-axis Voice Fingerprint and live voice audition make the writer's style an input.
- Six starting points feed one three-stage workflow: Talk it through → Outline → Draft.
- Inline AI and six review tools expose suggested changes before the writer applies them.
- One finished article fans out into channel formats, exports, and a deliberate GitHub publish.

## Outro / punchline
“Your voice. One coherent argument. Ready to publish.” BlogForge v0.10 — A workshop for long-form writing.

## User flow worth showing
Choose a starting point → shape material into an outline and one full draft → review visible suggestions → repurpose, export, or publish.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: an editorial product film about craft, judgment, and flow
- Interpretation: confident typography, real product surfaces, purposeful motion, and restrained claims

## Format: landscape — 1920×1080
## Duration: 24 seconds

## The nine-question rubric
1. **What is the app?** A local-first, multi-user long-form writing workshop.
2. **Most impressive claim?** “A workshop for long-form writing — that sounds like you, not a language model.”
3. **Visual hook?** A generic generate button physically giving way to a connected workshop.
4. **Actual UI to show?** Voice Fingerprint, six mode picker, three-stage draft flow, editor review rail, repurpose/export/publish.
5. **Shortest satisfying video?** 24 seconds; the whole app needs six compact but readable beats.
6. **Tone?** Polished; editorial product film.
7. **Audio?** Steady, clean music with four restrained interaction/impact cues.
8. **Share caption?** See canonical line below.
9. **User flow?** Voice/material → outline/draft → review decisions → distribution.

## Share copy (draft)
BlogForge is a workshop for long-form writing: start with your voice and sources, shape one coherent argument, edit with the decisions visible, then repurpose or publish when it is ready.

## Audio direction
- Role: steady professional bed with sparse motion-matched accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`
- Music treatment: restrained bed, fade in over 0.75s, fade out over final 0.75s
- Music cue guidance: 109.96 BPM preset; major locks at 8.74s, 18.56s, and 22.93s; sequential reveals use the 0.55s grid when readability allows
- Audio-reactive treatment: none; the product motion carries the film
- SFX posture: four cues only
- Audio-coupled moments: hook displacement, editor reveal, publish confirmation, final lockup
- Restraint rule: no per-card sound barrage and no sound that implies autonomous publishing

### Scene 4 — Edit with judgment — 5.8s

Sequential/interaction: select → compare → apply.
Audio intent: a soft reveal cue at the review beat.
Transition mood: leftward cut-the-curve → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Finish and distribute — 4.8s
A finished article gets an abstract hero, fans out through Atomize all, shows Markdown/HTML/Word exports, and resolves a deliberate Publish to GitHub action.

Audio intent: card fan and restrained success cue around 20.2s.
Transition mood: leftward cut-the-curve → Scene 6

### Scene 6 — The whole workshop — 3.0s
Voice, stages, manuscript, review, and publish artifacts file into one settled product card. Final line and BlogForge v0.10 hold.
Sequential/interaction: meaningful product pieces assemble into the lockup.
Audio intent: final chime at the 22.37s beat and music fade.
