# BlogForge Frame System

```yaml
colors:
  canvas_blue: "#e6efff"
  canvas_mint: "#e3f7f4"
  canvas_warm: "#fff4e0"
  ink: "#15224a"
  cobalt: "#2f6bff"
  teal: "#16c2b3"
typography:
  ui: "Inter"
  editorial: "Lora"
  metadata: "JetBrains Mono"
```

- Frame: 1920×1080 with 96px safe margin.
- UI panels: 24–34px radii, white at 82–94% opacity, navy borders at 10–16% opacity.
- Editorial headlines: 64–94px Lora, compact leading, maximum two lines.
- UI labels: 18–26px Inter; metadata: 14–18px JetBrains Mono, uppercase with tracking.
- Motion: one leftward current across scene seams; staged in-scene reveals; no idle breathing.
- Copy must remain readable in settled poses. Short labels hold at least 0.8s where they are meant to be read.
- Product surfaces should look like one workshop, not six unrelated slides.
